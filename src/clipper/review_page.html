Live channels

Language: English · change

Watches the streams while they are live and makes a draft (a first version of a clip) each time something exciting happens.

Channels that are not live yet are checked every half minute. Clipping starts as soon as they go live. Add only creators whose campaign you joined (a campaign is a paid clipping job), or who gave you permission.

Saw something good that the chat did not react to? Press Clip the last right after it happens. The draft appears in a minute or two, at the top of the list in My clips.

    Language

    Language: English

    Applies to clips made from now on. The narrator voice in explainer videos (the version where a voice introduces the clip) speaks English. Twitch, live streams and video files are always listened to on this PC.

    Keys for paid services, optional

    A key is a long password that a paid service gives you on its website. Paste it here once and press Save. It is kept only in a file on this computer, is never shown again, and goes to nobody but that service. Nothing here is needed: the program works without any key.

    With your own Claude plan you need no Claude key: sign in on the Set up page instead.

    Disk space

    Counting…

    Clearing removes a clip's video files, not its place in the list. A rejected clip that was cleared cannot be played or brought back to review. A finished clip whose download was cleared still plays, but its videos cannot be made again with a new look. A deleted clip waits in the bin (My clips, Bin) before it is gone for good.

    AI for titles and ratings optional

    Claude writes the opening text and titles, judges each clip, and can read a whole stream. Without it everything still works, but the texts are basic ones.

    Narrator voice for explainer videos

    The voice that introduces the clip in an explainer version. The free voice runs on this computer. ElevenLabs sounds more natural, but it is a paid service and needs your own key.
